Zero Feed Privacy Policy

Zero Feed does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, accounts, location tracking, contacts, or behavioral profiling.

App preferences and simple usage statistics such as session count and pages scrolled are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer.

In-app purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. Zero Feed does not receive or store payment card information.
